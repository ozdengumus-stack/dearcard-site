Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

1. Data Collection

Cardish does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to its own servers. All information entered by the user is stored locally on the user's device only.

2. Sender Name

Users may optionally enter a sender name when sharing a card. This name is stored only on the device and is never transmitted to Cardish's servers or shared with any third party.

3. Card Content

Cards created in Cardish are generated entirely on the user's device. Cardish does not view, store, or process any card content.

4. Third-Party Services

Card sharing is handled through the user's chosen messaging or social media applications. The privacy policies of those applications are outside the scope of Cardish's responsibility.

5. Contact

For questions regarding this privacy policy, please use our contact form.
